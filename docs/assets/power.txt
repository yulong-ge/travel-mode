  Battery profile — select changes for one atomic pmset update


  Battery profile: these values apply to battery power. They do not change the current AC state until that power
  source is selected.

   ▐X▌ lowpowermode: 0 → 1
     Meaning: Reduce CPU/GPU frequency and brightness to save energy.
     Values: 0=off, 1=on                                                                                             ▅▅
     Cost: On saves energy but reduces performance.

   ▐X▌ sleep: 1 → 5
     Meaning: Sleep after N idle minutes.
     Values: 0=never
     Cost: Shorter time saves energy but interrupts idle work sooner.

   ▐X▌ displaysleep: 10 → 3
     Meaning: Turn off the display after N idle minutes.
     Values: 0=never
     Cost: Shorter time saves energy but turns the screen off sooner.

   powernap: 0 → 0
     Meaning: Periodically wake during sleep for background updates.
     Values: 0=off, 1=on
     Cost: Off saves energy but delays updates and backups.
